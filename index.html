Privacy Policy

Tailor Bills — Digital Tailoring Receipt, Billing & Khata App

App Name: Tailor Bills Package: com.tailor.bills Developer: techbrothers Effective Date: October 30, 2026

1. Overview

techbrothers ("we", "our", or "us") built the Tailor Bills mobile application (Android Package ID: com.tailor.bills) as an ad-supported utility application for tailors, boutiques, and stitching shops. This page informs users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal and device information when using our Service.

Summary: All your shop records, customer bills, measurements, and rate lists are stored 100% locally on your device. We do not operate any cloud server to collect or store your business or customer records. Third-party advertisements are served via Google AdMob.

2. Information You Enter in the App (Local Storage Only)

To provide core tailoring billing and receipt management features, the app allows you to create and store:

  • Shop Profile Details: Shop name, tagline, owner name, phone/WhatsApp number, shop address, and receipt terms.
  • Customer & Order Records: Customer name, phone number, cloth description, measurement notes, stitching items, rates, advance paid, and delivery dates.
  • Stitching Rate List: Custom or default tailoring items and prices.

All of this data is saved strictly inside your device's internal SQLite/Room database. techbrothers cannot access, view, or transmit your customer database or financial records to any external server.

3. App Permissions & How They Are Used

Tailor Bills (`com.tailor.bills`) requests only the minimum permissions necessary for its features:

a) Read Contacts (android.permission.READ_CONTACTS) — Optional

When creating a new bill, you may optionally tap the "Pick from Contacts" button to select a customer from your phone's address book so you do not have to type their name and phone number manually. We only read the single contact you select in the system picker. We never upload, sync, or share your contact list with anyone.

b) Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE)

Used to load and display banner and interstitial advertisements through the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob).

c) Saving & Sharing Receipts (MediaStore / FileProvider)

When you choose to save a bill receipt image to your phone's gallery, generate a PDF invoice, print a receipt, or share a receipt via WhatsApp, the app uses Android's built-in MediaStore and FileProvider APIs to save or share the generated file at your direct request.

4. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

This app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to keep the app free for users.

Google AdMob and Google Play Services may automatically collect and process certain device and usage information in order to provide personalized or non-personalized ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance. This data may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location (country/city level)
  • App interaction and ad impression/click events
  • Device model, operating system version, and crash/diagnostic logs

Please review the privacy policies of these third-party service providers used by the app:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob & AdSense Partner Policy
  • How Google Uses Information from Sites or Apps That Use Our Services

Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising at any time on your Android device by opening Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) and selecting "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization".

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all shop profiles, customer bills, and rate items are stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data at all times:

  • You can edit or delete any individual bill or rate item directly inside the app.
  • You can clear all stored data at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Tailor Bills → Storage → Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its local database from your device.

6. Data Security

We value your trust in using Tailor Bills. Since your business data stays on your device, its security depends on your device's built-in Android security (such as screen lock, encryption, and OS permissions). Any data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS.

7. Children's Privacy

This application is a business and shop management tool intended for tailors and business owners and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
